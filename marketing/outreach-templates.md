### Targeting tips
- Find MSPs via LinkedIn (title: "MSP", "Microsoft 365 consultant", "M365 admin", "IT managed services") and local IT-services directories.
- Personalize with one true detail: a recent post of theirs, their city, a service they list.
- Track sends in a simple sheet (name, company, channel, date, reply). Stop messaging anyone who says no.
- Best converters: small MSPs (5–50 clients) — big enough to value the white-label, small enough to reply to a founder.
